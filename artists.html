Meet Our Talented Artists

Aarohi Desai

Painter, Visual Artist

Aarohi paints vivid landscapes inspired by Indian folk tales and rural settings. Living with hearing impairment, she uses expressive colors and textures to share her silent yet powerful vision.

View Gallery Portfolio

Raghav Iyer

Sculptor, Ceramics

Raghav crafts detailed clay sculptures that reflect Indian cultural themes. After his diagnosis with multiple sclerosis, he embraced art as a medium of emotional healing and connection.

View Gallery Portfolio

Priya Mehta

Digital Artist, Illustrator

Priya creates magical digital worlds using assistive tech. Despite limited mobility, she brings inclusive and fantastical realms to life that celebrate Indian mythos and futuristic imagination.

View Gallery Portfolio

Arjun Verma

Musician, Composer

Arjun is a sitar virtuoso blending classical ragas with ambient melodies. Visually impaired since birth, his deep sensitivity to sound transforms his compositions into spiritual journeys.

Listen & Shop Portfolio

Neha Sharma

Author, Poet

Neha pens evocative stories and poems in Hindi and English, touching on identity and inner journeys. As a neurodivergent writer, she brings a raw honesty and lyrical beauty to her words.

Read Excerpts Portfolio

Vikram Rao

Mouth Painter, Abstract Artist

Vikram paints abstract masterpieces using his mouth after a spinal cord injury. His work reflects resilience, using vibrant colors to communicate energy, motion, and emotion.
