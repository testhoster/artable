Our Story

Building a platform where art and accessibility create opportunity

Who We Are

ArtAble was founded in 2023 with a simple yet powerful mission: to create an inclusive marketplace where artists with disabilities can showcase their incredible talents and connect directly with art lovers worldwide.

What began as a small community project has blossomed into a thriving platform representing hundreds of artists across multiple disciplines—from visual arts and sculpture to music, literature, and digital creations. We believe that art transcends physical limitations and that every artist deserves equal opportunities to share their unique perspective with the world.

Our team is made up of artists, accessibility advocates, and technology specialists who work together to ensure that ArtAble remains not just accessible, but truly empowering for creators of all abilities.

Our Mission & Values

At ArtAble, we're driven by the belief that artistic expression should be accessible to everyone—both creators and appreciators alike. Our platform exists to break down barriers and create economic opportunities for artists with disabilities while connecting them with a community that values their work.

Inclusivity

We design every aspect of our platform with accessibility in mind, ensuring that artists of all abilities can participate fully and independently.

Economic Empowerment

We're committed to fair compensation practices, ensuring artists receive the majority of proceeds from their sales while providing them with business tools to thrive.

Community

ArtAble fosters meaningful connections between artists, patrons, and advocates, creating a supportive ecosystem for creative growth.
